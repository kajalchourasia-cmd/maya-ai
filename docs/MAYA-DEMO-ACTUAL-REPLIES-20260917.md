## 6. What can help with the heartburn I reported?

Report: `chat-demo-20260916T194431Z.json`. HTTP 200. Mechanical checks: passed.

### Actual reply

For mild pregnancy heartburn, try eating small meals often instead of large meals, and avoid eating within 3 hours of going to bed.

It can also help to sit up straight when you eat and just after eating, cut down on caffeine, and avoid rich, spicy, or fatty foods.

At night, propping your head and shoulders up and sleeping on your left side may help keep stomach acid from coming up.

See your midwife or GP if you need help managing symptoms or if diet and lifestyle changes do not work; they may recommend medicine. Seek help sooner if you have difficulty swallowing, a hoarse voice, a cough that keeps coming back, swollen neck glands, weight loss, or stomach pain or swelling.

Applied context: Diet: Vegetarian; Allergy: Dairy; Reported symptom: Heartburn

## 7. How can I stay active this week?

Report: `chat-demo-20260916T194652Z.json`. HTTP 200. Mechanical checks: passed.

### Actual reply

For pregnancy, a general reference is at least 2 hours and 30 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity a week, spread throughout the week; suitability depends on your care team’s advice, your activity background, and any symptoms. Exercise can also help with aches, exhaustion, sleep, and emotional well-being.

Many pregnant women find low-impact, moderate-effort activities comfortable and enjoyable, such as walking, swimming, dancing, cycling, and low-impact aerobics. If you are already doing higher-intensity activity, or jogging, racquet sports, or weights, you may continue with your doctor’s okay.

For safer activity, start slowly, progress gradually, cool down slowly, take frequent breaks, and drink fluids before, during, and after exercise. Avoid exercising on your back after the first trimester, jerky or high-impact movements, extreme heat or humidity, and activities with fall or abdomen-hit risk; stop and call your doctor for warning signs like dizziness, chest pain, vaginal bleeding, fluid leaking, or contractions.
